SAMPLE DOCUMENT — CLIENT SERVICES POLICY

1. CANCELLATION POLICY
Clients may cancel their service agreement within 14 calendar days of signing without penalty. Cancellations after this period are subject to a 30-day notice period. All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to the client services team.

2. DATA RETENTION
Client data is retained for a minimum of 5 years in accordance with regulatory requirements. After this period, data is securely destroyed. Clients may request a copy of their data at any time by contacting the data protection officer.

3. SERVICE LEVELS
Standard service requests are processed within 2 business days. Urgent requests flagged as priority are processed within 4 hours during business hours (08:00–17:00, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays).

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
All information provided by clients is treated as strictly confidential. Staff are bound by confidentiality agreements and may not disclose client information to third parties without written consent, except where required by law.

5. COMPLAINTS
Clients who wish to lodge a complaint should contact the client services manager in writing. All complaints are acknowledged within 1 business day and resolved within 10 business days. Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the managing director.
